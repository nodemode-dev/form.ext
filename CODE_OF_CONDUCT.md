# Code of conduct

form.ext is the Form family's extension studio, built in the open so that anyone can make extensions for the Form
apps. This page says how we treat each other while building it. It applies to this repository, its issues, pull
requests, discussions and wiki, and to anyone speaking for the project elsewhere. Extensions are reviewed and
discussed in [form.ext-registry](https://github.com/nodemode-dev/form.ext-registry), which follows the same code.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all. Many people come here to make their first extension.
- Argue about code, designs and ideas, never about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly and specifically.
- Credit the work of others, including the starters and examples you build on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, on any grounds.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption of any kind.
- Using the studio, its starters or its agents to make extensions meant to harm the people who install them, and
  sharing them here.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code by opening an
[issue](https://github.com/nodemode-dev/form.ext/issues), or by reaching the maintainers on GitHub: the
[nodemode-dev](https://github.com/nodemode-dev) organisation, [@nodemode-ai](https://github.com/nodemode-ai) and
[@gbarnai](https://github.com/gbarnai). A harmful extension is a security matter: report it as
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and decide what is fair: a private word, a public note, removal of content, or
a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. People who report in good faith will not face retaliation.
